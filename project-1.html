Co-Founder — Medusa Events

Building a live entertainment company in Vancouver from the ground up, with four equal partners and no external funding.

Overview

Medusa Events is a Vancouver-based event management and live entertainment organization that I co-founded alongside three other partners. We structured it as an equal shareholding arrangement from the start, which meant every operational and financial decision required clear processes and alignment across all four of us. There was no one person in charge, so the systems we put in place had to be tight enough to hold things together without constant oversight.

My work covered vendor negotiations, venue logistics coordination, and building the revenue distribution framework that governed how the company allocated earnings across equal partnership stakes. I also led the effort to reduce our operational footprint by sourcing event materials through local small business vendors rather than large suppliers, which lowered costs and cut post-event waste. Running a company this way from scratch taught me more about operational structure and financial transparency than most coursework does.
