Financial Manager — Stonegate Moving Solutions

Setting up a complete financial system for a summer moving enterprise, starting from nothing and staying compliant with BC regulations throughout.

Overview

Stonegate Moving Solutions was a summer moving enterprise where I held full responsibility for the financial and operational infrastructure. When I came on, there was no system in place. I built the bookkeeping setup from scratch using QuickBooks, established the accounts payable process, and put invoice coding and receipt verification workflows in place so that every transaction had a proper paper trail from day one.

On a weekly basis I monitored bank activity for irregularities, and at the end of each month I performed full bank ledger and credit card reconciliations to make sure the books matched reality. I also handled the regulatory side, navigating British Columbia's compliance requirements to keep all documentation in order and maintain version control throughout the season. A moving business runs on tight margins, and one untracked expense or missed reconciliation item can distort the whole picture.
