Accounting Assistant — Crowe MacKay

A contract placement supporting corporate finance operations at a public accounting firm, focused on ledger review, variance analysis, and multi-tier financial documentation.

Overview

Crowe MacKay is a public accounting firm where I completed a contract placement supporting the corporate finance team. The work was detail-intensive and operated on tight standards. My primary responsibilities involved reviewing corporate ledgers, verifying transactional records against source documentation, and performing variance analysis on large financial data sets to flag discrepancies before they reached the reporting stage.

A significant part of the role was managing complex documentation workflows and cross-referencing multi-tier ledgers for executive review. The environment reinforced a habit I had already developed elsewhere: every number needs to trace back to something. Working inside a structured public accounting team also gave me exposure to how professional firms organize their review processes, which is a different operating context from running your own financial systems independently.
